Peanut Butter Cookies

Description of above picture includes peanut butter cookies arringed in a cool pattern

Ingredients
  • 1 cup peanut butter(smooth or crunchy)
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg
Steps to follow to make this fucking delicious fucking cookie extravaganza
  1. Preheat your oven to 350*F(175*C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Mix the peanut butter, sugar, and egg together in a bowl until a soft dough forms.
  3. Roll the dough into small balls, place them on the baking sheet, and press a fork on top to make a crisscross pattern.
  4. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes until the edges are golden
