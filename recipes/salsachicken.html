Salsa Chicken & Melted Cheese

Salsa chicken & Melted Cheese picture

Ingredients
  • 1 boneless, skinless chicken breast (about 6 oz)
  • 3/4 cup salsa (your favorite brand)
  • 1/4 cup shredded cheddar or Mexican blend cheese
Steps
  1. Prehead the oven to 375*F(190*C) and place the chicken breast into a small greased baking dish.
  2. Spoon the salsa generously over the top of the chicken.
  3. Bake for about 20 minutes, then scatter the shredded cheese over the chicken and return the oven until the cheese melts and the chicken reaches an internal temperature of 165*F(74*C).
